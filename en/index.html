BRAND STATEMENT
Make manufacturing
WANPAKU.

For centuries, the world has advanced by making the same things in large quantities.
But today, that model has reached its limits.

Every person's body is different. So is every space, every purpose, every way of use.
Why should the tools we use all be the same?

Manufacturing was always meant to be made for someone.

wanpak fuses artisanal craft with digital fabrication
to realize manufacturing optimized for people and their environments.
Not mass production. Not one-of-a-kind.
To the right person, the right thing, in the right amount.

We are reclaiming manufacturing
as a fundamentally human act.

NAME ORIGIN

The Name

wanpak
01 — One Pack

From concept and design to manufacturing and service — the idea of executing the entire making process as one integrated package, without fragmentation.

02 — Wanpaku (わんぱく)

A Japanese word for the spirited, curious energy of a child at play — trying, making, failing, and trying again. We bring that same free-spirited energy into our work.

Crossing boundaries of technology and domain, drawing in collaborators, creating new value.
wanpak is a blend of "One Pack" and "wanpaku" — it is our very attitude toward making things.

SINCE 2020
FOUNDER STORY

Founder Story

Joe Arai
Founder & CEO

Throughout my career, I have worked in product development across diverse fields — automotive, EV, mobility, 3D printing, and medical devices.

One consistent challenge I observed was this: products optimized for the people who truly need them are simply not reaching them.

When design, manufacturing, and service are delivered as one integrated whole — only then can value be truly maximized for the individual needs that mass production cannot address.

wanpak was built to realize that vision.
We are implementing a new OS for manufacturing — building a world where the right tools reach the right people.

"Crafting Tools, For Each Person."

Every person has unique needs that off-the-shelf products cannot satisfy. By fusing artisanal craft with digital technology, we optimize every tool to fit the purpose and environment of the individual who uses it. VALUES

Our Values

01

Start from the individual and the place

An athlete's gear, a piece of furniture in a room — its value grows in connection with the person's body, the space it inhabits, how light enters, how hard the floor is, the surrounding atmosphere. What we pursue is not a product that is perfect in isolation.

02

Make nothing unnecessary

Make only what is needed, for those who need it. That, as a result, produces nothing unnecessary. Our responsibility to the environment is not a goal in itself — it is the natural consequence of making things the right way.

03

Design for what comes next

The moment of completion is not the end. Bodies change. Ways of living shift, little by little. Margin is the space for your future self to make adjustments. We design with that in mind from the start.

04

Cross every boundary

An insight from sport can transform furniture design. We don't root ourselves in any single industry — because belonging nowhere means we can bring answers that no single domain could generate on its own.
